A Chesstory study is not meant to be a copy of a general chess study room.

The starting point is a single reviewed game. The study should preserve the important positions, the explanation, the candidate lines, and the questions that mattered during the review.

That gives the space a different center of gravity. It is less about collecting material and more about turning confusion into a usable record.

The first useful version should support:

- a clear title for the reviewed game
- sections that divide opening, middlegame, and critical moments
- notes attached to the board and move tree
- visibility settings for private, unlisted, and public sharing
- a link that opens the exact review context

The beta will stay honest about what is finished. If a sample review is not real yet, it should not be presented as proof. The product earns trust by showing what exists and explaining what comes next.
