After a loss, it is tempting to ask the engine for every mistake. That gives a long list, but it does not always explain the game.

A better first pass is to find the moment where your plan stopped making sense.

Ask:

- What did I think I was playing for?
- What did the position actually demand?
- Did I miss a tactic, choose the wrong plan, or choose the right plan in the wrong move order?
- Which engine line proves the difference?
- What lesson would have helped before the mistake?

Chesstory's review format is being built around those questions. The aim is not to make every loss pleasant. The aim is to make the next similar position more readable.
