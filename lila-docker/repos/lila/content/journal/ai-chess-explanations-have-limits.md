AI text can sound confident even when the chess is thin.

That is why Chesstory should keep engine evidence close to every explanation. If a claim says one move is better than another, the page should still show the evaluation, depth, candidate line, and the position being discussed.

The goal is not to replace calculation with prose. The goal is to make the prose accountable to chess evidence.

Good explanations should admit their shape:

- what the move directly changes
- what the best move does differently
- what strategic idea the position revolves around
- what line supports the claim
- what uncertainty remains when the margin is small

This is also why beta wording matters. Chesstory can promise a serious attempt to explain games. It should not promise perfect coaching, guaranteed improvement, or private human judgment from a model that does not know your whole chess life.
