Bd7 ↔ Re8

Re8 moves the rook away from the later capture. In the legal variation Re8 Rf2 Ng4 Bxd5 cxd5, that rook cannot be captured by the same rook. After Bd7 Rf2 Ng4 Bxd5 cxd5, that same central capture is legal, but White can take the rook with Rxf8+. This is a conditional difference; the central capture need not be chosen.
